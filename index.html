    Numbers used

    • On-prem costs rise 3% a year. The SaaS fee rises 5% a year. Servers and people you still run rise at the on-prem rate.
    • With SaaS: 80% less infrastructure, 40% less ops effort, 80% less upgrade effort.
    • Year 1 adds a one-time move, 6 months running both, and 20% contingency on the move and the overlap only.
    • Upgrade days are staff time unless you tick contractors or overtime. Risk items are not added to the savings.

    Sources: Cooper Fitch UAE Salary Guide 2025; BMC Helix SLA, recovery, compliance, and service-location pages; Uptime Institute Annual Outage Analysis 2025. Server cost, cuts, and the move cost are our own careful estimates.

    Sources

    Defaults sit on a public figure where one exists. Where it does not, the number is our own careful estimate. Nothing here is a customer result.

    1. Engineer cost. Cooper Fitch, UAE Salary Guide 2025. Monthly pay, before bonus. Technology: Cloud Engineer AED 24,000–30,000; DevOps Engineer AED 23,000–30,000. We use AED 28,000 a month × 12 × 1.25 loading = AED 420,000. The 1.25 is ours. Cooper Fitch PDF
    2. Availability. Helix subscription services, 99.9% in a month. Planned maintenance does not count as downtime. BMC Helix service level agreement (PDF)
    3. Recovery. BMC states a 15-minute recovery point and a 4-hour recovery time for BMC Helix services, unless the contract says otherwise. Business continuity and disaster recovery
    4. Certifications. ISO 27001, and a yearly SOC 2 Type II examination, as described for BMC Helix services. Compliance
    5. Data location. Data stays in the country of the service location you select. Backups for a UAE OCI subscription are stored in Abu Dhabi (UAE Central). Confirm the site in your contract. Service locations
    6. Outage context, not a Helix figure. Uptime Institute, Annual Outage Analysis 2025: 54% of respondents said their most recent significant, serious, or severe outage cost more than US$100,000, and one in five said more than US$1 million. Uptime executive summary (PDF) · Press release

    Our own careful estimates, with no public price: servers per solution, yearly server running cost (this excludes the hardware purchase), hardware refresh, the 3% and 5% rises, the 80/40/80 effort cuts, migration cost, months running both, year-1 contingency, upgrade timing and days, working days, and the default team size.
